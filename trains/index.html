How it works

Every dot is a train that is moving right now. The positions come from Irish Rail’s realtime feed, pulled by a small FastAPI service running in Docker on a server in my house and served back out through a Cloudflare tunnel. Nothing here is precomputed — the page asks for positions when you open it, and again every minute while you watch.

The island is drawn from Natural Earth boundary data, projected with an equirectangular fit scaled by the cosine of the mean latitude. Over a country this size the distortion is invisible, and it means the map needs no tile server, no API key and no mapping library — it is a single SVG path with points placed on top.
