About

I’m a full stack engineer based in Dublin, with a computer science background from Trinity College Dublin. I work across the stack — APIs and services through to the interfaces on top of them.

Outside of work I build things for the fun of it: generative sketches, hardware side projects, and tools that scratch my own itches. A few of them are below.

Sketches

Small generative pieces, written in p5.js and running live in the browser.

  • Random Walk

    A circle randomly chooses a direction — up, down, left or right — every frame. Each time it passes through a cell in the grid that cell’s opacity increases, so the path it has taken slowly emerges.

Parametric design

Generative and parametric 3D models, published openly and printed by people I’ve never met.

  • 13 models
  • 6.5k downloads
  • 3.6k prints
  • 3.6k likes

  • Infinite Unique Snowflake Generator

    A parametric model that produces a different snowflake on every generation, so no two printed copies are alike. The most downloaded thing I’ve published.

  • Modular SKÅDIS shelving

    A parametric system of pegboard shelves and LEGO display frames, sized from a handful of variables so the same source produces the whole family of parts.

  • Topology-optimised parts

    Modelled in SolidWorks, both by hand and — for one part — using topology optimisation to strip material the load path didn’t need while keeping the part stiff enough to print and use.
