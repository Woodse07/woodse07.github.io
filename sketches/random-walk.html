Random Walk

p5.js

Started paused because your system is set to reduce motion. Press play to run it.

What it does

A circle starts at the centre of a grid and picks a direction — up, down, left or right — at random every frame, rejecting any move that would take it off the grid. Each cell records how many times it has been visited, and draws itself more opaque the more often it is crossed. The path the walker has taken emerges from that, with the most-travelled routes near the centre showing brightest.

The leading circle cycles through hues as it goes, so the current position stays visible against the accumulated trail.
